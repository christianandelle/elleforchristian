To Christian.

Some letters are worth waiting for.

My dearest Christian,

If you’re reading this, then there are probably a thousand things I wish I could still say to you in person—or over one more phone call.

Sometimes I still laugh when I think about how we met. Out of all the people on that website, you were my very first match. Who would’ve thought that one random conversation would lead me to someone who would become my safest place?

Thank you for finding me.

You know what I love most about you?

It’s your heart.

I’ve always admired how deeply you love your family. Every time you talk about your dad, I can hear it in your voice. Sometimes you even cry, and every single time, I quietly fall in love with you all over again. Not because you’re emotional, but because it reminds me how genuine your love is.

Your dreams have never been selfish.

You never told me you wanted success just so you could have more. You wanted it because you wanted your mom, your dad, and your brothers to finally experience the kind of life they deserve. That’s the kind of man you are.

And that’s why I’ve always believed in you.

You amaze me more than you know.

I still can’t get over how you remember the smallest details from years ago. You can tell stories from grade school as if they happened yesterday. I used to listen to you in complete amazement, wondering how someone could remember so much. Then I’d remind myself…

“Of course he can. He’s Christian Rey.”

It also makes perfect sense why you were a valedictorian.

You’re brilliant.

But what I love most isn’t how smart you are.

It’s the person you are underneath all of that.

Thank you for letting me know your family.

I loved hearing you call your mom.

I loved hearing your younger brothers.

I loved hearing you talk about Northern Samar.

I loved getting to know your amazing dad and your loving mommy through the stories you shared with me.

I still pray that one day everything becomes okay between you and your older brother because I know how much family means to you.

One of my favorite moments was simply listening to you pray.

I didn’t always understand every word, but somehow, I always understood your heart.

You probably don’t know this, but whenever you prayed, I would become quiet because I never wanted to interrupt something so beautiful.

You made faith sound peaceful.

I’ll also never let you forget how much I secretly hated whenever you teased me for sounding so conyo.

You’d imitate me saying, “Talaga?” or “Really, huh?” and I’d get annoyed while you laughed even harder.

Looking back now…

I think I’d give anything just to hear you tease me one more time.

Thank you for letting me sing Happy Birthday to you.

It wasn’t a grand celebration.

It was just us.

But somehow, that’s what made it perfect.

Christian…

There is something I’ve been carrying in my heart for a long time.

I was afraid to tell you because I kept thinking that if I did, I’d only become another weight on your shoulders.

You already carry so much.

Your dreams.

Your responsibilities.

Your family.

Your future.

I didn’t want my illness to become one more thing for you to worry about.

So I kept it to myself.

Not because I didn’t trust you.

But because I loved you.

Maybe I was wrong.

Maybe I underestimated how much you would have wanted to walk through this with me.

If I did, I’m sorry.

You deserve honesty, and you deserve to know that none of this was because I didn’t love you enough.

It was because I loved you so much that I kept wishing I could protect your heart.

If there’s one thing I hope you’ll always carry with you, it’s this:

Please keep loving life.

Please keep chasing your dreams.

Finish your board exam.

Build that customs company you’ve always talked about.

Give your parents the life you’ve dreamed of giving them.

Spoil your mom.

Take care of your dad.

Keep looking after your brothers.

Keep praying.

Keep laughing.

And please…

Don’t stop exploring the world.

You know how much I dreamed about seeing more of it.

So if one day you find yourself standing in front of a beautiful ocean, watching a sunrise, walking through a city you’ve never been to, or simply sitting somewhere peaceful…

Take a deep breath for me.

Smile.

And tell me about it in your heart.

I’ll always love the version of you who’s excited to share every little story.

Please don’t ever lose that.

I know you don’t like cockroaches.

I know you secretly enjoy teasing me.

I know you’re impatient sometimes.

I know you can be stubborn.

And strangely enough…

I loved all of those little imperfect parts too.

Because they were yours.

Thank you for every late-night call.

For every study session you let me quietly listen to.

For every prayer.

For every laugh.

For every ordinary day that somehow became extraordinary because you were in it.

You once told me that people come into our lives for a reason.

If that’s true…

Then I think you came into mine to teach me what gentle love feels like.

If, years from now, you ever hear a song that reminds you of me, or you remember the sound of my voice, I hope it doesn’t make you cry for too long.

I hope it reminds you that there was someone who loved you with her whole heart.

Someone who believed in you before you even realized how incredible you were.

Someone who never stopped praying that every dream you whispered during those late-night calls would one day become your reality.

And if that day comes…

I hope I’ll somehow still be the first person you think of telling.

I love you, Christian.

More than you’ll probably ever know.

And if there’s one thing I hope stays with you forever, it’s not my sadness.

It’s my love.

Because that has always been the truest part of me.


— Elle

P.S. Turns out a heart doesn’t have to be whole to love completely. Stay safe, always.
